## Research Interests

- Category Theory
- Homotopy Theory

<p style="text-align: justify; text-justify: inter-word; line-height: 1.6;">
  My current research focuses on reformulating classical theorems using the Kock-Møller model of negative sets (cf. <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19437" target="_blank" style="color: #e63946; text-decoration: underline;">Signs in objective linear algebra, exemplified with exterior powers and determinants</a>), aiming to further develop the model and provide a deeper understanding of its cancellation structure through concrete examples.
